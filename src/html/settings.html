Import from the original Quick Dial

Brings over your tiles with their exact grid positions, folders, pictures, grid and style settings. Bookmarks already in your Quick Dial folder are reused; missing ones are created.

  1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, find the original Quick Dial and click Inspect.
  2. Copy the export snippet and paste it into the Console tab (type allow pasting first if Firefox asks). It saves quickdial-export.json to Downloads.
  3. Choose that file with Import from file… above.
